## Why the 20-minute test

The Tatra Loop needs long, steady climbing power, not short bursts. A 20-minute effort
measures exactly that. A ramp test would over-estimate your FTP, because you have a
strong aerobic base and a weaker sprint. Over-estimated zones make every sweet spot
session too hard.

## Before the ride

- Sleep and eat normally. No hard session on Mon Aug 17 — easy Z2 or rest only
- Check the morning brief. HRV below the 36–48 ms baseline or a poor night → move the test
- Eat 60–80 g carbs about 2 h before. Nothing heavy
- Pick the calmest hour of the day. Wind is the biggest source of error outdoors
- Tyre pressure and chain as normal — this becomes your reference ride
- Zero-offset the 4iiii before you start (Edge → sensor menu → calibrate)

## Edge setup

Use a dedicated screen with four fields:

- **3-second average power** — your steering wheel during the effort
- **Lap average power** — the number that actually becomes your result
- **Elapsed lap time**
- **Heart rate** — a check only, not a target

Do not use instant power. It jumps too much to pace with.

## Warm-up (~35 min)

1. 10 min easy Z2 spin
2. 3 × 1 min at 100+ rpm, easy gear, 1 min easy between — wakes the legs up
3. 5 min easy
4. 5 min hard, around your best guess at test power — this clears the first-effort shock
5. 10 min easy, fully recovered before you start

Skipping step 4 is the classic mistake. The first hard effort of any day always feels worst.

## The test

Press **lap** and hold for 20 minutes on the flattest, most open stretch you have. No
junctions, no lights, no coasting.

- **Minutes 0–3:** hold back. Start at ~200–210 W. It must feel too easy
- **Minute 5:** check lap average. If it feels sustainable, add 5–10 W
- **Minutes 5–15:** hold the number. Breathing is heavy but rhythmic. You can say 3–4 words
- **Minutes 15–18:** this is where it hurts. Defend the number, do not raise it
- **Minutes 18–20:** empty the tank

Expect average HR in the 150s, climbing past 165 near the end. Observed max HR is ~174–175
(per [atlas-insights.md](atlas-insights.md)).

Press **lap** again at 20:00. Then spin easy for 10–15 min.

## Reading the result

- **FTP = 20-minute average power × 0.95**
- Also record **Normalized Power ÷ Average Power** for the lap. Under **1.05** means you
  paced it steadily. Above 1.10 means you surged, and the number under-reads your true FTP
- W/kg = FTP ÷ 72 kg

If the second half was clearly stronger than the first, you paced too softly. Add ~5 W to
the result, or re-test in a week.
