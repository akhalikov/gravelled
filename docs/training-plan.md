# Training Plan — Tatra Loop 1-Day (Season Goal)

**Target**: Tatra Loop in 1 day, autumn 2026 (late Sep – mid Oct). 196 km, ~2,700 m climbing, est. 10–10.5 h moving / ~12.5 h total. Pacing: HR 120–130 on the flats, controlled on climbs, eat 60–80 g carbs/hour.

**Rehearsal**: 2-day Tatra Loop with overnight (Liptovský Mikuláš area), during Aug 8–16 window. ~100 km / ~1,350 m per day. Counts as Q3 bikepacking trip + route recon + gear shakedown (tubeless, fit position, fueling).

## Weekly Structure (Aug–Sep, after the 2-day trip)

- **1× intensity ride** — progression of sweet spot / tempo (the muscular endurance that climbs need):
  - Weeks 1–2: 3×12 min tempo (done once already ✓)
  - Weeks 3–4: 2×20 min sweet spot (88–94% FTP once zones are set)
  - Weeks 5–6: 3×20 min sweet spot or 2×30 min tempo
  - Sprints workout (already prepared): rotate in every 2nd week as a lighter session, not extra
- **1–2× easy rides** — Z2, HR ≤ 130, Vistula loop pace deliberately relaxed
- **1× long ride, progressive** (the key sessions):
  - ~60 km with hills
  - ~80 km
  - **100–110 km with 1,000+ m climbing — the Grizl century, goals.md milestone**
  - 130–150 km (dress rehearsal: same food, same start time as the real thing)
  - Taper week → **Tatra Loop 1-day**
- **Gate intensity by morning brief**: HRV below baseline or poor sleep → swap intensity for Z2 or rest. Evening intensity rides hurt REM/HRV — prefer earlier when possible
- Low-cadence climbing blocks (60–70 rpm, seated) on 1–2 easy rides per month — Tatra climbs are long, not steep

## On-the-bike fueling practice

Practice on every long ride, not on event day: 60–80 g carbs/h from hour one, drink to thirst with electrolytes, one real meal stop mid-ride. The engine is proven — long days end via stomach and contact points, not legs.

## Event-day sketch (1-day attempt)

- Start 6:00 from Zakopane, counter-clockwise
- First 3 h deliberately too easy (HR ≤ 125)
- Two 40-min stops (Liptovský Mikuláš ~km 90, Poprad side ~km 150) + short water refills
- Daylight budget: ~5:45–19:30 by late Sep — no margin for a late start
